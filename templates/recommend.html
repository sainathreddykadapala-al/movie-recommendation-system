Machine Learning Algorithm Mechanics
1. TF-IDF Vectors

Overview, director, cast, genres, and keywords are cleaned and transformed into 5,000-dimensional sparse feature vectors with stop-word filtering.

2. K-Means Clustering

Unsupervised K-Means groups the 4,800 movies into 12 thematic clusters, narrowing down candidate pools efficiently.

3. Cosine Similarity

Measures the angular cosine similarity between feature vectors to rank top 10 recommendations with full metadata explanations.
